Privacy Policy

How the Windows app and this website handle information.

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Files and folders you choose

Findexy searches locations you select. To make search work, it reads file and folder names, paths, types, sizes, and dates. It saves an index and app settings on your PC. Favorites, Collections, and saved searches are also stored locally.

Document contents and OCR

If you enable content indexing in Findexy Pro, the app extracts searchable text from supported files. Optional OCR can read text from supported images and scanned PDFs. These operations run on your PC. The indexing service does not upload your files, filenames, paths, search terms, or extracted text to Shrez Labs.

Support and diagnostics

If you choose to contact support, your email provider sends the message and anything you enter or attach. Findexy's built-in report can add app and version diagnostics. It does not automatically add filenames, paths, search terms, results, or document contents. Review your message before sending it.

Microsoft Store purchases and updates

Microsoft Store handles Findexy Pro purchases, ownership checks, and app updates. Payment details are handled by Microsoft under its own terms and privacy policy. Findexy checks whether your Microsoft account owns the Pro add-on to unlock the related features.

This website

This is a static website. It has no account form, advertising, analytics script, or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process normal connection data when you visit it. The Microsoft Store link takes you to Microsoft's website.

Your choices

You can change indexed locations, turn off content indexing and OCR, and reset app preferences in Findexy. Turning off content indexing removes extracted text from the local index. For questions about information you sent to support, email us.
